## The scope of the role

The work sits close to the life of the church. It includes creative campaigns, churchwide initiatives, storytelling, worship experiences, communications strategy, and the organizational systems a team needs if that work is going to happen more than once.

Worship, communications, and production overlap. I lead them as one practice, with the people who plan, make, and carry the work.

## How I think about it

The useful questions come early. What is the church trying to say? Who needs to be involved? Does the team have a way to get there that does not depend on last-minute effort?

A campaign, a service, and a communication plan start to look like the same problem: a group of people trying to say something clearly, on time, in a way the church can actually feel.

I care about the craft of that work, and about the leadership that keeps it from depending on one person's memory.
